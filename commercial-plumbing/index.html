When a business plumbing problem is an emergency

A restaurant with a backed-up kitchen line, a retail store with a flooded restroom or an office with a burst supply pipe cannot wait for a regular appointment. Health codes, safety and lost revenue all push the same way. The first goal is to stop the water and restore the fixtures that keep the doors open.

Emergency commercial plumbing in Vernon Hills is built around fast triage. We ask what has failed, whether a shutoff is reachable and what has to stay open, and then plan the repair around your hours. Many businesses prefer after-hours work, which we price in advance.

What to do while you wait

  • Close the main water valve or the valve for the affected area.
  • Stop using the affected fixtures and put out wet-floor signs.
  • Move stock, files and equipment away from standing water.
  • Note the time, the fixtures involved and any photos for your insurer and landlord.
Common calls

Common commercial plumbing calls

The calls we hear from businesses are different from residential ones, because the same pipe serves many people at once.

Kitchen drain and grease line backups

Grease, food solids and wipes collect in kitchen lines and interceptors. A slow floor sink is often the first warning.

Restroom overflows and clogged mains

Heavy use and the wrong items in toilets stop commercial drains faster than a house.

Water heater failures

Larger tanks and recirculation loops fail in ways that stop dishwashing and hot water to staff areas.

Burst or leaking supply lines

Pressure, age and freezing in unheated spaces can open a line quickly.

Backflow assembly problems

A failed test or a leaking assembly can stop water service or trigger a compliance notice.

Grease interceptors and kitchen lines

Lake County Public Works, which serves Vernon Hills, has a sewer use ordinance that requires food service businesses to have a grease removal system. As we read it, the system must be at least 500 gallons, floating material must be removed at least every 90 days and the unit must be pumped fully on a set schedule, with no interior units allowed.

Plumbers do not replace a pumping service, but we keep the lines to and from the interceptor flowing, check baffles and inlets that are failing, and jet the line when grease has built up. Confirm the current rules with Lake County Public Works at 847-377-7500 because ordinances change. Our hydro jetting page describes the cleaning method, and the drain cleaning page covers smaller clogs.

Backflow prevention and testing

A backflow preventer is a valve assembly that keeps contaminated water from flowing back into the public supply. Businesses with irrigation, fire sprinklers, boilers, restaurants or commercial dishwashers often have one, and many are required to be tested on a schedule.

Rules for testing and who may test vary by the water supplier and the type of device. We can install, repair and replace assemblies, and we help you find out what applies to your building by checking with the water provider. Our plumbing installation page covers new fixtures and devices.

Retail, restaurants and offices around Vernon Hills

Vernon Hills has a busy retail and dining corridor along Milwaukee Avenue and Route 60, with shopping near Hawthorn Mall and the Mellody Farm area. Many tenants share buildings, so a clog in a shared line affects neighbors and the landlord needs to be involved early.

We coordinate with property managers when a shared line is involved, document the cause with camera footage and give you notes for the lease or insurance. If the shared line is a sewer problem, our sewer line cleaning page shows how we find the blockage.

Water heaters, restrooms and fixtures

A commercial water heater serving a kitchen or a salon carries a heavy load. When it fails, we look at repair, replacement and whether a second unit makes sense. Our water heater replacement page has the permit and sizing details.

Restroom fixtures, flush valves, urinals and faucets in public use fail from wear. We repair and replace them, keep common flush valve parts in the truck and schedule larger fixture upgrades outside business hours.

Code and permits note

Commercial work often involves more permits and inspections than home work. Call the Village of Vernon Hills building department at 847-367-3700 about your building, and ask whether the guidelines for your use, such as a restaurant, add requirements.

Illinois plumbers are licensed through IDPH, which offers a license lookup, so you can check anyone you hire.
