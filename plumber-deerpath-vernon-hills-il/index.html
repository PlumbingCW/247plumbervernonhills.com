What sources say about Deerpath

Two real-estate sources describe Deerpath as a single-family area with 1,262 houses on about 340 acres. One lists build years from 1974 to 2021, and an older sponsored profile gives an average build year of 1977.

The sources place it differently. One says it lies south of Route 60 and Townline Road and east of Butterfield Road. Another says it is off Route 45, west of Milwaukee Avenue and east of Route 83. Treat the edges as approximate and confirm your address on a map.

Streets that appear in listings and sales include South Deerpath Drive, Albert Drive, Monterey Drive, Annapolis Drive, Montebello Drive, Abilene Lane and Lindon Lane.

Five things to find before you call

Deerpath is large and its homes were built over a long span, so the questions below matter more than the street name.

1

The main water shutoff

Know where the valve is and whether it turns. In a burst-pipe moment, that is the first job.

2

The water heater label

The data plate shows the model and often the manufacture date. Read it out to us if you can.

3

The cleanout

If the house has a sewer cleanout, note where it is. It saves time on a drain problem.

4

Any past backups

Tell us about earlier clogs or backups. A repeat problem points to a different cause than a first one.

5

Your floor drain

Note whether a basement floor drain is present and whether water has ever come up through it.

Public mains and your own line

Lake County Public Works serves water and sewer in Vernon Hills, and it lists an emergency line at 847-377-7188 for utility problems. If neighbors on your street have the same backup, that is the number to try first.

A problem that affects only your house usually starts on your own side of the connection. For the pipe from the street to your meter, our water line repair page explains the work. The wider list of places we answer calls is on the service area page.
