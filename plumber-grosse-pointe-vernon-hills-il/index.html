One name, several sections

Real-estate sites list Grosse Pointe, Grosse Pointe Village, Carriages of Grosse Pointe and Grosse Pointe Circle as separate entries in Vernon Hills. A village zoning map also shows street names such as Grosse Pointe Boulevard. The map does not label subdivisions, so it does not tell us how those entries relate.

Grosse Pointe Village is described as single-family homes by Centex Homes, built in the late 1980s and early 1990s on Route 83 and Route 45. Listings mention North Fiore Parkway, Saugatuck Trail, Shelby Court, Julie Lane, Napier Court and Southfield Drive.

A 2014 sponsored article describes Carriages of Grosse Pointe as 252 townhomes by Centex from about 1993, and Grosse Pointe as single-family homes from the 1990s west of Oakwood Road and south of Route 45.

How to give an address no one can mistake

When a name covers more than one section, the safest details are the street, the house number and whether you own a house or a townhome. Add the nearest cross street if you can.

For a townhome, say whether the problem is inside your unit or in a shared area. A leak that starts in a shared wall or a common line is usually a question for the association first. For a single-family house, tell us where the water is and what you have shut off.
