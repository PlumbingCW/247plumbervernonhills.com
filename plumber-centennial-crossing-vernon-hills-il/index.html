What is documented about Centennial Crossing

A real-estate profile describes Centennial Crossing as 191 detached single-family homes on about 86 acres, built by Town & Country Homes. It is west of Milwaukee Avenue off Route 45, and a Metra station sits across from the main boulevard.

The same profile lists build years of 1997 to 2001, alley-loaded lots with attached or detached garages, and about ten floor plans with three to five bedrooms. Streets in its listings and sales include Ranney Avenue, Depot Street, Superior Street, LaSalle Street and Huron Street.

Wikipedia lists a Centennial Crossing residential development among the village's annexations of 1986. That is an annexation date and not a build date, which is why the two sources do not conflict.

Alley lots and where things are

On an alley-loaded lot the garage faces the alley, and the front of the house faces the street. Utility connections can sit on either side, so where your water shutoff, meter and sewer cleanout are is worth finding before you need them.

When you call about a drain, tell us whether the problem is in the house, the garage or outside. If a garage has a utility sink or a floor drain, mention it. Those small details help us ask the right questions on the phone.
