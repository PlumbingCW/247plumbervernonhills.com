Attached homes: who is responsible

In a townhome, some lines belong to you and some belong to the association. The split is set by your declaration and your association's rules, not by us. Read them before you call, or ask your management office.

Before any problem happens, find your shutoff. Many attached homes have the main shutoff in a utility area, a basement or a crawl space, and some have a separate valve for each fixture. Locating it now saves minutes later.

A leak that crosses a shared wall affects a neighbor, so tell them and your management quickly. If the water is coming from a line inside your own unit, closing your shutoff and calling a plumber is usually the right first move. Our burst pipe repair page lists the steps.
