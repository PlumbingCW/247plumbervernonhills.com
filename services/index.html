Emergency or planned: how we sort the calls

Emergency calls are the ones where water, sewage or gas is already causing damage, or a family has no hot water. Those get dispatched first, at any hour. Planned work, such as a remodel, a tankless upgrade or a water softener, is scheduled around your calendar.

Both kinds start with the same phone number. We ask what you see, tell you what to shut off and give a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are offered when available for problems that fall between the two.

Where to start if you are unsure

No hot water usually points to water heater repair or water heater replacement. Slow or backed-up drains start at drain cleaning, and a wet ceiling or a high water bill starts at leak detection.

If water is coming up a basement floor drain, read our sewer line cleaning page, and if a pipe has split, go straight to burst pipe repair. Businesses can start at commercial plumbing.
