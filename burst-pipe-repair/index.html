A burst pipe is an emergency

Water from a split pipe soaks insulation, drywall, flooring and belongings quickly, and wet materials start growing mold in a day or two. Every minute with the water on adds damage. Shutting off the water is the first thing to do, before you call anyone.

Emergency burst pipe repair in Vernon Hills starts on the phone. We confirm where the water is coming from, talk you through the shutoff and send a technician to stop the leak and repair or replace the damaged section.

What to do right now

  • Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the basement.
  • Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, and open an upper one to help air in.
  • Turn off the breaker for any room with water near outlets, fixtures or the ceiling.
  • Move belongings out of the way and take photos for your insurer before cleanup.
Causes

Why pipes burst

Not every burst pipe comes from winter. These are the main causes we see in Lake County homes.

Freezing

Water expands as it freezes and splits pipe walls, often in exterior walls, garages, crawl spaces and unheated rooms. The split appears when the ice melts.

Corrosion and age

Old galvanized or thin copper pipe thins from the inside until it fails.

High pressure

Water pressure well above normal stresses joints and fittings.

Impact or work

A nail, a drill bit or a shifting joist can puncture a pipe.

Joint failure

Poor soldering or old fittings let go under pressure.

Frozen pipes: what to do before they burst

A pipe that has frozen but not yet split can often be saved. Signs are no water at one faucet, frost on an exposed pipe or a cold wall. Keep the faucet open and warm the area with a space heater placed safely, a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use an open flame.

If you find a split pipe or water appears as it thaws, close the main valve and call. Lake County winters bring deep freezes, so garages, exterior walls and unheated basement corners deserve attention before the cold. Insulating exposed pipes, sealing drafts and letting a faucet drip on the coldest nights help prevent bursts.

After the water stops

Once the leak is stopped, drying matters more than anything. Remove standing water, run fans and a dehumidifier and open affected walls if they stay wet. Mold can start in a day or two, so speed counts.

We repair the pipe and test it under pressure. We do not handle the full restoration, which usually involves a water damage company, but we document the failure with photos and notes for your insurer. For hidden moisture after a repair, leak detection can help confirm that nothing else is leaking.

Insurance, shutoffs and being prepared

Sudden pipe bursts are often covered by homeowners insurance, while slow leaks and wear usually are not. Your policy will state what applies, and the first step is to document everything. Ask your insurer about water backup coverage too.

Everyone in the house should know where the main shutoff is and how to close it. If your valve is stuck or leaks, replace it before an emergency. Our water line repair page covers the line between the street and your meter, and a sump pump helps with groundwater rather than pipe water.
