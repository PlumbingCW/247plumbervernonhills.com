Local knowledge

What Vernon Hills homes and businesses should know

Vernon Hills is a village in Lake County, incorporated in 1958, with about 26,850 residents at the 2020 census. Tap water comes from Lake Michigan through Lake County Public Works, and the sanitary sewer is run by the same department in its South Central service area.

That matters in an emergency. The pipe from your house to the public sewer is yours to keep clear, while the main belongs to the utility. If neighbors have the same backup, Public Works lists an emergency line at 847-377-7188.

Permits

The village building department, at 290 Evergreen, publishes a permit guide for water heater and sewer work. Call 847-367-3700 to confirm today's rules before you start.

Licensed plumbers

Plumbers are licensed through IDPH, which offers a license verification lookup. Check anyone you hire, including us.

Retail and offices

Businesses around Hawthorn Mall, Mellody Farm and Corporate Woods call about grease lines, restrooms and water heaters, often after closing. See our commercial plumbing page.

Neighborhoods

We work across subdivisions such as Centennial Crossing, Hawthorn Club, Grosse Pointe Village and Muirfield Village. Tell us your street when you call so dispatch can route the nearest crew.
