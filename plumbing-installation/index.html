What we install

Plumbing installation covers anything new: a kitchen or bath remodel, a basement bathroom, a laundry hookup, a utility sink, a new water line to an outbuilding or an appliance connection. Good installation starts with a plan for supply, drain, vent and access.

We work with homeowners, contractors and designers. We read plans, check what the walls and floors will allow and give a clear price before work starts. Changes midway are discussed before they happen.

Gas lines and backflow devices

Gas lines for ranges, dryers and outdoor appliances must be sized and tested correctly. A licensed plumber runs the pipe, tests for leaks and, where required, arranges the inspection. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility and 911 before you call anyone else.

Backflow preventers protect the public water supply from contamination and are required in many irrigation and commercial setups. We install, repair and replace them, and we help you find out what testing applies. See our commercial plumbing page for business needs.

Permits, licensing and inspections

The Village of Vernon Hills has a published permit guide, and the building department at 847-367-3700 answers questions about what a given job needs. Inspections are on weekdays and are requested ahead of time, so we plan around them.

Plumbers in Illinois are licensed through IDPH, which offers a plumbing license verification lookup. We encourage you to check any contractor, including us. We also coordinate with other trades on remodels. If an existing pipe fails during the work, repiping or leak detection may come into the plan.

Code and permits note

New plumbing usually needs a permit and an inspection. Ask the Village of Vernon Hills building department at 847-367-3700 what applies, and know that the guidelines the village follows are based on the Illinois State Plumbing Code.

Illinois plumbers are licensed through IDPH, which offers a license lookup, so you can check anyone you hire.
