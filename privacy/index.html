Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

What this site collects

This website does not have forms and does not ask you to submit personal information. We do not run accounts or sell anything online. The way to reach us is by phone or email.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, for security and reliability. The embedded Google Maps on our pages may set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Phone calls

When you call, we collect the phone number you call from and the details you give us about the job, such as your address and the problem. We use them to dispatch a plumber, to give you a price and to follow up on the work.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We tell callers if a recording notice applies, and you can ask us not to continue if you do not agree.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply. We do not add you to marketing lists without asking.

Sharing

We share job details with the plumber assigned to your call and with vendors that help us run the business, such as phone and hosting providers. We do not sell your information. We may disclose it if the law requires.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. Write to contact@247plumbervernonhills.com or call (847) 281-8626.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
