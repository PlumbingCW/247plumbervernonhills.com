Sewer backup: what to do first

A sewer backup means the main drain that carries waste away from the house is blocked or overloaded. Water that cannot leave the house has to go somewhere, and it comes up at the lowest drain, usually a basement floor drain, a shower or a toilet. That makes it a health hazard as well as a plumbing problem.

Stop all water use, including the toilets, because every flush adds to the problem. Keep children and pets away from the backed-up area. Water that has touched sewage should be treated as contaminated, and anything porous it reached may need to be discarded.

Checklist while you wait

  • Do not run the washing machine, dishwasher or showers.
  • Turn off power to a flooded basement only if you can reach the panel safely from dry ground. Otherwise stay out and call your electric utility.
  • Take photos of the damage for your insurance company before any cleanup.
  • Ask the neighbors if they have the same problem. If they do, the blockage may be in the public sewer main and Lake County Public Works lists an emergency line at 847-377-7188.

Is it your pipe or the public main?

Vernon Hills is in the South Central service area of Lake County Public Works, which maintains the public sanitary sewer. The county's sewer use ordinance makes the owner responsible for keeping the building sewer, the private pipe from the house to the public main, clean and flowing freely.

In practice, a backup that affects only your home points to your own line. A backup on several homes on the same street points toward the main. If you are unsure, we can check from your cleanout with a camera or cable, and tell you what the footage shows before you decide who to call.

How we clean a sewer line

Most main lines are cleaned with a drum cable machine, which feeds a flexible steel cable with a cutting head through the cleanout. The head chews through roots and soft blockages while the cable is rotated. For grease, scale or repeat roots we follow with a hydro jet, which our hydro jetting page explains.

We then run a camera down the line to confirm it is open and to look at the pipe itself. A line that is clean but damaged will clog again, so the footage tells us whether to stop at cleaning or recommend work on the sewer line repair page. You can ask to see the recording, and our sewer camera inspection page explains what the camera shows.

Storm drains, yard drains and catch basins

A private storm drain, yard drain or catch basin is separate from the sewer. It carries rainwater, not waste, and it clogs with leaves, silt and debris. We clear these with a cable or a jetter and check that the discharge point is open.

The distinction matters. Lake County Public Works prohibits connecting downspouts, foundation drains and groundwater sump pumps to the sanitary sewer. If your yard drain or sump pump has been connected to the sanitary line, we will explain what to change. This is not storm drain cleaning for municipal systems, which is handled by the village or the county.

Septic questions in a sewered village

Vernon Hills homes connect to the sanitary sewer, so septic systems are uncommon here. If your property is on a septic tank, pumping is a different trade, and we will tell you on the phone whether we can help or who to call instead.

If your home is in Mundelein or another nearby town, the sewer arrangement may differ, since Lake County Public Works serves only portions of Mundelein and some neighboring areas. Ask us and we will confirm what applies at your address.
