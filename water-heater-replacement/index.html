When a failed water heater is an emergency

A leaking tank can put dozens of gallons on a basement floor in a short time, and a tank that has gone cold in January leaves a family without hot water for dishes, bathing and laundry. Those are the two situations that call for emergency water heater replacement in Vernon Hills.

On the phone we ask about the heater's age, fuel, size and location, because that lets us arrive with the right unit. Same-day replacement is offered when available, though a specialty unit or a heater that needs a new venting layout may take longer, and we tell you that before you commit.

What to do while you wait

  • Close the cold supply valve on top of the tank so it stops filling.
  • Turn the breaker off for an electric heater or set a gas heater to off.
  • Clear the floor around the heater and keep children and pets away from standing water.
  • Photograph the leak and the rating plate for your insurer and for sizing the new unit.

Tank, tankless or heat pump: which fits your home

The right choice depends on your household size, fuel, the space around the heater and how long you plan to stay. Moving from gas to a heat pump unit, or from a tank to tankless, changes the venting, the electrical or gas supply and sometimes the drain, so we confirm those before quoting.

Standard tank heater

  • Lowest purchase price and the simplest install.
  • Hot water is limited by tank size, usually 40 to 50 gallons for a typical family.
  • Works with existing gas or electric hookups in most homes.
  • Typically lasts around eight to twelve years.

Tankless or heat pump

  • Tankless gives continuous hot water but needs correct gas line sizing and venting.
  • Heat pump units use much less electricity but need air space and a condensate drain.
  • Higher purchase price, longer service life in many cases.
  • Our tankless water heater page covers flushing and repair.

What replacement day looks like

1

Confirm the unit

We check the rating plate, fuel, venting, the shutoff valves and the discharge piping before anything is removed.

2

Drain and remove

The old tank is drained, disconnected and carried out. The area is cleaned and a drain pan or discharge path is checked.

3

Install to code

A new temperature and pressure relief valve with a metallic discharge pipe, a cold shutoff, a gas shutoff or an electric disconnect, and an expansion tank where the system needs one.

4

Test and explain

We fire the heater, check for gas and water leaks, verify draft on gas units and show you the controls and the drain valve.

Permits and inspections in Vernon Hills

The Village of Vernon Hills publishes a permit guide for water heater replacement. As of that guide, a replacement permit is issued over the counter, requires a final inspection, and calls for a completion bond. The guide lists a new temperature and pressure relief valve, metallic discharge piping, a shutoff valve on the cold line and a gas shutoff or electric disconnect as requirements.

It expects a licensed plumber to do the work unless the owner lives in the single-family home.

The guide also says plumbers must furnish a copy of their Illinois plumbing license and contractor registration, and that tankless units do not need a permit. These documents were published a few years ago, so treat them as a starting point and confirm the current rule with the building department at 847-367-3700 before work begins.

Inspections are weekdays only, and the guide asks callers to request them by 3:30 pm for the next business day. A replacement done on a Friday night will usually be inspected the following week, which is normal and does not affect hot water.

Sizing and fuel: getting the right heater

A heater that is too small runs out in the middle of a shower, and one that is too large wastes fuel keeping extra water hot. Size by peak hour demand rather than by the number of bedrooms. The first-hour rating on the yellow EnergyGuide label is the number to compare.

Gas heaters need a flue that drafts properly and a gas line sized for the burner. Electric heaters need a dedicated circuit at the right amperage, and heat pump units need a nearby condensate drain and enough open air. If your heater sits in a basement near a floor drain, a drain pan piped to that floor drain is cheap protection against the next leak.

If you are weighing a planned upgrade rather than an emergency swap, our water heater installation page covers new locations, fuel changes and code items in more detail.

Hard water and the next heater

A new heater is a good time to deal with what shortened the last one. Vernon Hills water is treated Lake Michigan supply, and the annual report we reviewed gives no hardness number, so test your tap or ask Lake County Public Works. If the water proves hard, a softener or a yearly flush protects the new tank, and the water softener and filtration page explains what to look for.
