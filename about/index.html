About Vernon Hills 24/7 Plumbing

We are a plumbing company based at 325 North Milwaukee Avenue in Vernon Hills, Illinois, built around one idea: when plumbing fails, someone should answer the phone.

What we do

We handle emergency and planned plumbing for homes and small businesses in Vernon Hills and the nearby Lake County towns. That covers water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, burst pipes, sump pumps, fixtures and more. Our full list is on the services page.

The phone is answered 24 hours a day, every day. When you call, we ask what you see, tell you what to shut off and explain what happens next.

How an emergency call goes, step by step

You call (847) 281-8626 and a person answers. We ask where you are, what you can see and whether anyone is in danger. If water is spreading, we tell you how to close the main valve and which breakers to switch off.

Then we dispatch a plumber and give you what we can on timing. We do not promise an exact arrival, because traffic and weather vary. When the plumber arrives, you hear the price before work starts, and you decide.

Local sources we rely on

Plumbing rules and utilities are local, so our guides and service pages use local sources. Lake County Public Works supplies the water and runs the sanitary sewer in its South Central service area, which includes Vernon Hills and portions of Mundelein. Its sewer use ordinance sets the owner's duty to keep the building sewer clean and bars sump pumps and downspouts from the sanitary sewer.

The Village of Vernon Hills building department publishes permit guides for work such as water heaters and sewer repair. Those guides are a few years old, so we tell readers to confirm with the department at 847-367-3700. We treat IDPH as the licensing authority for plumbers in Illinois.

Who answers and who comes to your door

The people who answer the phone work with the plumbers who dispatch to your address, so the details you give do not get lost. We write down your street, what you have shut off and what the plumber should bring.

We do not publish names, photos or staff counts on this site, because we would rather show you how we work than print claims you cannot check. If you want to know who is coming, ask on the call.

What to ask any plumber

Ask for the price before work starts. Ask whether the work needs a permit and who pulls it. Ask to see the license, and check it through IDPH. Ask what is covered by a warranty, and get that in writing.

A good plumber answers plainly and does not rush you into a larger job. If something we say does not make sense, ask us to explain. We would rather spend two minutes on the phone than do work you did not understand.

How we work

We give a clear price before work starts. We tell you when a repair will hold and when replacement makes sense. We explain what we find, and we do not push a bigger job than the problem needs.

Where the village requires a permit, we handle it. The Village of Vernon Hills building department publishes permit guides, and we confirm today's rules before we start. Plumbers in Illinois are licensed through IDPH, which offers a plumbing license verification lookup.

What we do not claim

You will not find invented reviews, ratings or award badges on this site. We would rather you judge us by a phone call and a clear price than by a number we cannot back up.

Our guides use local sources, such as Lake County Public Works and the village permit guide, and we say when a source is a few years old.
