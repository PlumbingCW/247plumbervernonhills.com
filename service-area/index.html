Vernon Hills, our home base

Vernon Hills is a village in Lake County, incorporated in 1958, with about 26,850 residents at the 2020 census. We serve ZIP code 60061 from Milwaukee Avenue, with dispatch available around the clock.

Tap water comes from Lake Michigan through Lake County Public Works, which also runs the sanitary sewer in its South Central service area. Neighborhoods such as Centennial Crossing, Hawthorn Club, Grosse Pointe Village and Muirfield Village are all within our everyday route.

Dispatch runs from the Milwaukee Avenue base, so crews reach most Vernon Hills addresses by Route 21 or Route 60, and reach Mundelein along the same corridor.

Mundelein and the surrounding towns

Mundelein, ZIP 60060, is our nearest covered town and has a dedicated page. See emergency plumber Mundelein for local details, including the 2017 flood and what it means for basements.

We also answer calls in Libertyville (60048), Lincolnshire (60069), Lake Zurich (60047) and Buffalo Grove (60089), plus Long Grove, Mettawa and Indian Creek. Those towns do not have their own pages, and everything on our service pages applies to them.

Utilities and rules by town

Lake County Public Works serves Vernon Hills and portions of Mundelein, Mettawa, Indian Creek and Long Grove on the sanitary sewer. That means the answer to who maintains the main can differ from street to street, even inside one town.

If you have a backup and want to know who to call, start with a plumber who can run a camera from your cleanout. If neighbors are affected too, Public Works lists an emergency line at 847-377-7188. Permits are handled by each town's building department, and in Vernon Hills that is the office at 290 Evergreen, 847-367-3700.

What changes from town to town

The plumbing itself is the same, but the context shifts. Mundelein is lower and flatter around Diamond Lake and has a recent flood history, so sump pumps and basement water come up often. Our Mundelein page covers that in detail.

Libertyville, Lincolnshire, Lake Zurich and Buffalo Grove each have their own village offices and rules. We confirm local permit requirements before work that needs them, and we tell you what we find.

Commercial and retail areas

Businesses around Hawthorn Mall, Mellody Farm and Corporate Woods in Vernon Hills, and along Route 60 and Route 45 into Mundelein, call about after-hours leaks, grease lines and water heaters. Our commercial plumbing page explains how we plan that work around opening hours.

Shared buildings add a wrinkle, because a clog may affect several tenants. We document the cause with camera footage so the landlord and tenants can sort out who is responsible.

Seasons and the calls we expect

Winter brings frozen and burst pipes, and water heaters fail when the demand is highest. Spring and summer storms bring sump pump failures and basement water. Fall is a good time to flush a water heater and check shutoff valves before the cold.

Whatever the season, the 24/7 line is the same number. See our burst pipe repair and sump pump pages for the two most weather-driven calls.

What to tell us when you call

Give us your street, the nearest cross street and your town. Tell us what you see, what you have shut off and whether anyone is in danger. Those details let dispatch choose the right crew and tell you what to expect.

If you are not sure whether we serve your address, call and ask. See all of our plumbing services to learn what we can do once we arrive.
