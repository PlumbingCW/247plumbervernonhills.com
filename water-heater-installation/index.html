Installation versus replacement: which page applies

A like-for-like swap of a failed tank is replacement, and our water heater replacement page handles that urgent case. Installation is the planned version. You are moving the heater, changing fuel, adding a second unit, going tankless or choosing a heat pump model, or finishing a basement or addition.

The difference matters because planned work lets you make better choices. You can compare units, check the gas line or electrical capacity ahead of time, and schedule the permit inspection without a cold house deadline.

Scope

What goes into a proper installation

Most callbacks on water heater work trace back to the parts around the heater rather than the heater itself. These are the items we verify on every install.

Location and clearances

Combustion air, access for service, floor drain or drain pan, and distance from combustibles.

Venting

Atmospheric flue, power vent or direct vent, with the right pipe, slope, termination and support.

Gas or electrical supply

Gas pipe sized for the burner, or a circuit sized for the elements or compressor, with a disconnect.

Relief and discharge

A new relief valve piped to a safe termination in metal, so a discharge cannot scald anyone or flood the floor.

Expansion control

An expansion tank where the plumbing is a closed system, so pressure does not climb every time the heater runs.

Shutoffs and unions

A cold-line shutoff and unions so the heater can be serviced or swapped later.

Permits and Illinois licensing

In Illinois, plumbers are licensed through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the Village of Vernon Hills asks plumbers to furnish a copy of their state license and contractor registration with a water heater permit. The village's permit guide describes an over-the-counter permit with a final inspection for replacement, and lists the new relief valve, metallic discharge piping, shutoff and disconnect requirements.

Because the guide dates from a few years ago, we check current requirements with the building department at 847-367-3700 for new locations and fuel changes. You can also check any plumber's Illinois license yourself using the Illinois Department of Public Health's plumbing license verification lookup, and we encourage you to.

Gas line, venting and electrical: the hidden costs

The cost of a water heater project often hides in the parts you cannot see in the product listing. A tankless unit may need a larger gas pipe than the old tank because it burns fuel faster. A heat pump unit may need a new circuit and a condensate line to a drain. An old chimney may be oversized or damaged for a modern, more efficient appliance.

We look at each of these before quoting. If the project grows beyond a water heater, for example a rework of supply piping, the plumbing installation page describes how we handle larger scopes.

Code and permits note

The village permit guide describes what a new water heater must include, such as a new relief valve with a metal discharge pipe and a gas shutoff or electric disconnect. Those items follow the Illinois State Plumbing Code that the guide cites.

Ask your plumber which of them apply to your unit, and confirm the current rules with the Village of Vernon Hills building department at 847-367-3700.
