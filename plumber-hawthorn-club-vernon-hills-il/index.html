What sources say about Hawthorn Club

A real-estate profile describes Hawthorn Club as single-family homes built between 1988 and 1994 by United Homes. It places the neighborhood off Route 45, west of Milwaukee Avenue and east of Route 83. A second site gives 1989 to 1992 for the build years.

The two build-year ranges differ by a few years, so we show both. Listings mention Sheffield Lane, Montclair Road, Camden Place, Evergreen Drive and Richmond Lane. One site gives home sizes of about 1,898 to 3,024 square feet with three to four bedrooms.

A neighborhood park named Hawthorn Club Park appears in listing-site park pages. We do not know whether the park lies inside or beside the neighborhood.

Replacing a water heater: the permit step

The Village of Vernon Hills building department publishes a permit guide for water heater replacement. As of that guide, a replacement permit is issued over the counter, and tankless units are described separately. The guide is a few years old, so call 847-367-3700 to confirm.

Ask whoever does the work who pulls the permit. If you hire a plumber, check the license with the Illinois Department of Public Health before you agree to anything. Our water heater replacement page covers the work itself, and our water heater installation page covers new locations and fuel changes.
